# Prompt to give your AI

Copy everything in the block below into the AI you use most (the one that has seen your projects, code and chats). Paste its answer back to me and I'll fill in the site.

```text
I'm building a personal portfolio website and I need you to write a complete profile of me
from everything you know about my work. Use only facts you actually know from our
conversations, files and projects. If you don't know something, write "UNKNOWN" instead of
guessing. Don't flatter me or invent anything.

Return it in exactly this structure:

1. BASICS
   - Full name, preferred display name, college/company, city/country
   - Current role or title (e.g. "CS student", "full-stack developer")
   - Year I started coding and what first got me into it
   - Public email, GitHub, LinkedIn, X/Twitter, any other links

2. ONE-PARAGRAPH BIO (3 sentences max, first person, plain language)

3. ONE-LINE MOTTO or philosophy I keep repeating

4. PROJECTS (every one you know about, newest first). For each:
   - Title
   - Year (or "UNKNOWN")
   - Category tag (e.g. WEB APP, AI/ML, MOBILE, TOOLING, GAME, HARDWARE)
   - One sentence: what it does and who it's for
   - Problem it solves and why I built it
   - Tech stack (languages, frameworks, services)
   - My exact role (solo, team lead, contributor) and team size
   - Notable result: users, awards, metrics, shipped/not shipped
   - Links: repo, live demo, video (or "NONE")
   - Biggest technical challenge and how I solved it
   - Status: shipped / in progress / abandoned

5. NOW BUILDING: the one project I'm working on right now, in 6 words or fewer

6. SKILLS & TOOLS: each skill with the year I started using it and a level
   (learning / comfortable / strong), newest first

7. BEYOND CODE: hackathons, competitions, clubs, talks, open source, design, writing,
   volunteering, hobbies. For each: name, year, what I did, outcome.

8. EDUCATION & EXPERIENCE: institution or employer, role or degree, dates, highlights.

9. ACHIEVEMENTS: awards, certifications, rankings, publications.

10. PATTERNS: the 3 themes that run through my work, and 3 words that describe how I work.
    Base this only on evidence from my projects.

11. GAPS: list anything above that you marked UNKNOWN so I can fill it in myself.

Format the whole answer as plain text sections in the order above. No marketing language.
```

Tip: if your AI can't see your projects directly, paste your GitHub profile README, a list of repo names, or your resume first, then send the prompt.
